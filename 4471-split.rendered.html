#4471 was merged as one date bug with one fix queued. Run causes.ts over the merged reports and they are three different problems. The merge happened on the subject line (decision 0001), and subjects do not say why a date is wrong.

Not known, not one issue. Fixing the parser closes most of #4471. It does not touch the other two.

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant's dates come out flipped. This is the bug #4471 describes, and most of the reports are this one.
  2. hint. Tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first because the form tells them to, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The parser fix does not help. The form hint needs its own ticket.
  3. one-day move. The desk read these reports as a date off by one day, not a swap. The reports mention one month only, an invite that arrived before the booking, and the same meeting on two days for two cities. That looks like a time zone bug. They are filed under parser by tenant, so the table below counts them there.

Why intake got it wrong

intakeTag is a guess from the reporter's browser. The cause depends on the tenant: its locale and the booking form it runs. Read the tag as a hint about the browser, never as the cause.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is a count of reports, not a count of bugs. Check the cause before you merge.
  • When a decision says "merge on subject", add the check that would catch it being wrong.
